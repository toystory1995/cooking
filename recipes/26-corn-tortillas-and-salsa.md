Masa is a different material from wheat dough: no gluten, so it is held together by
gelatinised starch alone, and it tells you immediately when the hydration is wrong.

## Ingredients
**Tortillas:** 250g masa harina (nixtamalised corn flour — *not* cornflour or polenta) · about 320ml warm water · ½ tsp salt

**Salsa roja:** 4 tomatoes · 2 dried guajillo, 1 dried ancho · 2 cloves garlic · ¼ onion · salt

**Salsa verde:** 400g tomatillos · 2 serrano chillies · ¼ onion · 2 cloves garlic · coriander · lime · salt

## Method
1. **Masa.** Mix the flour, salt and most of the water and knead 2 minutes. Rest 20 minutes, then check the feel: it should be like soft play dough and, when you press a ball flat with your thumb, the edges should stay smooth. **Cracked edges mean it is too dry** — add water a tablespoon at a time. This test is the whole craft.
2. Roll 30g balls and keep them covered.
3. Press between two sheets of plastic cut from a freezer bag, to about 2mm.
4. **Comal, three flips.** Hot dry pan. Lay one on: 15 seconds, flip; 45 seconds, flip; then press the centre lightly with a cloth and it should inflate. That puff means the layers have separated and it will be soft. Straight into a cloth-lined basket — they steam each other and stay pliable.
5. **Salsa roja.** Toast the dried chillies in a dry pan for 20 seconds a side until they smell sweet, then soak in hot water 15 minutes. Char the tomatoes, garlic and onion in the same dry pan until blistered black in patches. Blend with the drained chillies and salt. Simmer 5 minutes in a little oil to bring it together.
6. **Salsa verde.** Char the tomatillos, chillies, onion and garlic the same way. Blend raw with coriander, lime and salt — no cooking. Taste them side by side: the same method, one cooked and one not, giving completely different results.

## What you're learning
Reading dough by feel rather than by recipe weight, since masa hydration shifts with the
brand and the humidity. Also dry-charring as a flavour technique that needs no fat at all.

## Push it further
Fry stale tortillas for totopos and chilaquiles. Then track down whole dried corn and lime
and nixtamalise your own — a full day, and the difference is not subtle.
