MasterChef increasingly asks for a vegetable to carry a plate. That means treating it like
a protein: seasoned early, browned hard, basted, rested, carved.

## Ingredients
- 1 large cauliflower, leaves left on
- 60g butter, 2 tbsp olive oil, 2 cloves garlic, thyme
- Salt, smoked paprika
- **Romesco:** 2 red peppers roasted and peeled · 2 tomatoes charred · 60g toasted almonds · 30g toasted hazelnuts · 1 slice bread fried in oil · 1 dried ñora or ancho chilli, soaked · 1 clove garlic · 2 tbsp sherry vinegar · 100ml olive oil · salt

## Method
1. Trim the base of the cauliflower flat but keep the leaves — they char and become the best part. Salt it all over, including down into the florets, and leave 30 minutes.
2. **Blanch** the whole head in heavily salted boiling water for 6–8 minutes. This seasons it through and starts the cooking, so the outside does not burn while the core is still raw.
3. Drain upside down thoroughly, then dry. Sit it in a tray, rub with oil and paprika, and roast at 200°C for 35–45 minutes.
4. In the last 10 minutes, add butter, garlic and thyme to the tray and **baste it repeatedly**, exactly as you would a chicken. It should be deeply browned in patches, with blackened leaf tips, and a skewer should meet no resistance at the core.
5. **Romesco.** Pound or blend the nuts, fried bread, garlic and soaked chilli to a coarse paste. Add the peppers and tomatoes, then the vinegar, then trickle in the oil while blending to a thick, slightly grainy sauce. It should be sweet, smoky, sharp and nutty in that order. Salt hard.
6. Rest the cauliflower 5 minutes, then carve it in wedges at the table like a joint. Spoon romesco alongside and finish with flaky salt.

## What you're learning
That vegetables want the same structure as meat — seasoning ahead, a par-cook, hard
browning, basting, resting — and that a nut-and-bread sauce is an emulsion held by starch
and ground nuts rather than egg or butter.

## Push it further
Do the same with a whole celeriac, a hispi cabbage cut in half, and a head of fennel. Then
build a plate where the vegetable is the centre and the meat is the garnish.
