The other great noodle soup, and the opposite philosophy to ramen: where ramen wants fat
and body, phở wants clarity and perfume. Same eight hours, completely different discipline.

## Ingredients
- 2kg beef bones — knuckle, marrow and a little oxtail
- 500g beef brisket or shin
- 2 large onions, halved; 1 large piece of ginger, halved lengthways
- **Spices:** 4 star anise · 1 cinnamon stick · 4 cloves · 1 tbsp coriander seed · 2 black cardamom · 1 tsp fennel seed
- 3 tbsp fish sauce, 1 tbsp sugar (yellow rock sugar if you have it), salt
- 400g flat rice noodles
- **To serve:** 200g raw sirloin sliced paper-thin · spring onion · white onion sliced thin · coriander · Thai basil · beansprouts · lime · fresh chilli · hoisin and sriracha on the side

## Method
1. **Blanch** the bones for 10 minutes in boiling water, then dump them out and scrub the pot and each bone under cold water. This is tedious and it is the reason phở broth is clear.
2. **Char** the onions and ginger, cut side down, in a dry pan or under a grill until blackened in patches. That char is a defining flavour, not a shortcut.
3. **Toast** the spices in a dry pan for 60 seconds until fragrant, then tie them in a cloth or a tea infuser so they can be pulled out.
4. Cover the bones with cold water, bring **very slowly** to a bare simmer, and skim relentlessly for the first half hour. Never let it boil — this is the same rule as the chicken stock, and it is what separates phở from a murky beef soup.
5. Add the charred aromatics, the spice bag and the brisket. Simmer at 85–90°C for **6–8 hours**, topping up with hot water. Pull the brisket out at 2–3 hours, once tender, and rest it in cold water to keep it pale, then slice.
6. Strain through muslin. Season with fish sauce, sugar and salt — taste it hot and taste it more than once. It should be clean, sweetly spiced and deeply beefy, with no muddiness.
7. **Assemble to order.** Noodles cooked and drained into a warmed bowl. Sliced brisket and raw sirloin laid on top — the broth cooks the raw beef as it hits it, so it must be boiling when it goes in. Onion and spring onion, then the broth poured over hard.
8. Herbs, sprouts, lime and chilli go on the table, not in the bowl. The eater finishes their own.

## What you're learning
Clarity as a technique — blanching, scrubbing, never boiling, skimming — held for eight
hours. Set against ramen, this teaches you that the same ingredients and the same time can
be pushed to opposite results purely by temperature control.

## Push it further
Make phở gà with chicken, which takes 3 hours and is much less forgiving of dull seasoning.
Then serve the two side by side and ask which broth carries further.
